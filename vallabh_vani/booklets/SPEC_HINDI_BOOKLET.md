# Hindi pravachan booklets (मूल-भाषा प्रवचन पुस्तिका) — brief and house style

A booklet of this kind prints one discourse of Gosvāmī Śrī Śyām Manoharjī **in the words and the idiom in which he spoke it**: not a translation, not a summary, not standard Hindi. The text is his speech, punctuated, divided into sections, with his quotations identified, sourced and indexed, preceded by the Sanskrit verses he composed for the occasion and followed by the badhāī pada sung at the end.

The brief was learned from the exemplar the owner shared on 2026-10-23: **प्रतिबिम्बवाद ( जीवात्माके स्वरूप पर विशेष प्रवचन )**, Vallabh Sukhdham, Gosvāmī Śrī Puruṣottamajī Prākaṭyotsava 2026, revision 2 (typed in Word by Atulya Sharma, printed with *Microsoft Print to PDF* on 2026-10-21; 92 pages, 5.5 × 8.5 in). Page numbers below are the exemplar's. Every typographic value was measured in the PDF (fonts read from the embedded font files, sizes and positions from the page content). Where the exemplar is inconsistent with itself, the rule here is the one to follow; the inconsistencies are listed at the end for the owner to decide otherwise. A clean Unicode copy of the exemplar, verified glyph by glyph against the PDF, is `exemplar_pratibimbavada_2026_rev2.md`, kept by the owner and in the Claude Project (not in this public repository; the opening pages are `samples/pratibimbavada_opening.md`).

## The standing rule: the speaker's words in the speaker's idiom

- Keep his sentences as he spoke them: his words, his Vraj- and Gujarati-coloured Hindi, his English, his repetitions, asides, jokes, stories and questions to the audience. Do not paraphrase, shorten, reorder or "improve". A sentence he abandons ends in `...`.
- **Do not standardise the dialect.** The exemplar writes हे 973 times and है only 7 (in a heading and in other people's quoted words), हें 219 times and हैं once; हतो/हती/हते for था/थी/थे; मोकु, आपकु, तुमकु for मुझे/आपको/तुम्हें; कियो, भयो, गयो, रह्यो, कह्यो for किया/हुआ/गया/रहा/कहा; सु for से; कु for को (dative); को/की/के as the genitive (जीवको परिमाण); अपन् for "we"; plural obliques in -न् (लोगन्कु, ग्रंथन्को, वादन्को); बखत for वक्त. `IDIOM.md` lists every form with its frequency and examples from the exemplar.
- People he quotes keep their own speech: the Jain muni speaks standard Hindi ("आग देखो खाती है कि नहीं खाती?"), the old Gujarati lady Gujarati ("गुरुजी, आपे आखु प्रवचन कर्यु पण टेबल तो आव्यू नहीं!"), and Dr Johnson's retort and the jibe "You are male chauvinist!" stay in English.
- Editing is limited to punctuation, paragraphing, division into sections, quotation marks, bold for quotations, and the source of each quotation. Drop only noise and pure disfluency (a stammered syllable, a word restarted at once).
- Sensitive remarks stay as spoken (the exemplar keeps the cockroach-and-Trump joke and "male chauvinist"). Never reproduce film-song lyrics; devotional padas and scripture are fine.
- The speaker's Sanskrit verses, his charts, the occasion and the badhāī pada are supplied by him or his office. Never compose, complete or "correct" them.
- The working draft may carry the editorial flags of the other series (`‹?›` uncertain, `‹…›` inaudible, `‹ ›` conjectural, `‹श्रवणाधारित›` as heard); the booklet that is delivered carries none. Each flag is resolved by listening, by the printed source, or by asking the owner; what stays unrecoverable is set as `...` and listed in the closing report.

### The ASR does not give you the idiom

Sarvam's `saaras:v4` in verbatim mode wrote the twelve Śāstrārtha classes of 2026 in standard Hindi: है 7,765 times against हे 2, किया/हुआ/गया against कियो/भयो/गयो about 1,100 to 6, मुझे 69 against मोकु 0; only a few forms of the idiom came through (वाको 17, अपन 207). Either the speaker used standard Hindi in those classes, or the recogniser normalises his idiom. **Before editing a new booklet, compare ten minutes of the recording with its transcript.** If the transcript has normalised his forms, the idiom can only be restored by someone listening (Claude may propose each restoration with the transcript line, for a listener to accept); never convert standard forms to dialect forms by rule.

## The booklet, part by part

1. **Title page** (p. 1, a recto): title, 36 pt bold (`प्रतिबिम्बवाद`); subtitle in round brackets with a space inside each bracket, 20 pt bold (`( जीवात्माके स्वरूप पर विशेष प्रवचन )`); lower, the venue and occasion in two centred lines, 16 pt (`वल्लभ सुखधाम · गोस्वामी श्रीपुरुषोत्तमजी` / `प्राकट्योत्सव २०२६`); lower still the speaker, 16 pt (`गोस्वामी श्याम मनोहरजी`). Folio 1 is printed.
2. **Blank verso** (p. 2, folio printed).
3. **Contents** (p. 3): `अनुक्रमणिका — <title>`, 13 pt bold, flush left; one bulleted item per section, 13 pt justified, `खण्ड N` in bold followed by ` — title`. The exemplar gives no page numbers. **Blank verso** (p. 4).
4. **The speaker's Sanskrit verses** (pp. 5–9): heading `प्रतिबिम्बादिवादीयाः उपक्रमश्लोकाः`, 18 pt bold, centred; verses 1–5, one half-verse per line, every line stretched to the full width so that the daṇḍa stands at the right margin, the verse number after the second half (`॥१॥`); a prose insert `तथाहि` followed by the twelve śruti and smṛti sentences the verses rest on, numbered `१.`–`१२.` inside one paragraph; verses 6–36; a closing pair, centred; the colophon in three centred lines (`इति गोस्वामिश्रीदीतितात्मजेन श्याम मनोहरेण / निर्मिताः / प्रतिबिम्बादिवादीयाः उपक्रमश्लोकाः`, as printed; दीक्षितात्मजेन is surely meant). On these pages paragraphs are not indented.
5. **The discourse** (pp. 10–89) in nine sections, `खण्ड १` to `खण्ड ९`, each beginning on a new page (pp. 10, 16, 23, 34, 40, 50, 62, 74, 86): six to twelve pages a section, far longer than the sections of the English editions. The heading is 16 pt bold, underlined, centred, one or two lines; on p. 10, the model opening, its first line stands 2.3 in from the top of the page and the text begins 4.1 in from the top. Titles name the topic in the booklet's own idiom, with a colon or dash for the sub-topic: `खण्ड ५ — शाङ्करमतके तीन पक्ष: प्रतिबिम्ब, अवच्छेद, वार्तिक`.
6. **The speaker's chart** (pp. 17–18), placed where he says "या चार्टकु देखोगे तो सब कुछ खुलासा हो जायगो": a line drawing (चेतना → आत्मवाद / अनात्मवाद …) filling p. 17, and its continuation, a typed tree with box-drawing characters in 11 pt, at the top of p. 18.
7. **Closing words** (p. 89), the speaker's own, ending with the blessing of the occasion.
8. **बधाई पद** (p. 90): `(वल्लभकुल बधाई पद)` in bold, then each line of the pada as its own paragraph, indented like body text.
9. **उद्धृत वचन** (pp. 91–92): heading 14 pt bold underlined, centred, low on the page like a section opening; one numbered item per quotation, 11 pt: Devanagari number, the opening words of the quotation in bold within quotation marks, the source in brackets, a dash and the page number in Devanagari digits (10 pt), in order of occurrence. The exemplar lists 24 items.

Not in the booklet: timestamps, speaker labels, editorial flags, figures of our own, footnotes, a summary, running heads.

## Orthography and punctuation

| Matter | Rule | Exemplar |
|---|---|---|
| Sentence end in Hindi | Latin full stop `.` | `पर वा बखत कुछ भी नहीं समझ पड़ती हती.` (the 27 `।` and 110 `॥` all stand in Sanskrit) |
| Daṇḍa | `।` and `॥` only in Sanskrit; always the character ॥, never two `।` | `तेजस्त्वात्॥` |
| ? ! , ; : | Latin marks, no space before | `ये जीवको छूटनो क्या हे? अटकनो क्या हे?` |
| Dash | em dash `—`, unspaced in the text, spaced in headings (`खण्ड १ — …`) | `आत्मवाद जब आवे माने—there is a substance called Aatma` |
| Unfinished sentence | `...` (the exemplar has three to five dots) | `कोई ठिकाने चेतना हे.....` |
| Quotation marks | double for speech and texts, single for a word or a short phrase; curly in print (write straight quotes in markdown; the build curls them) | "गुरुजी, आपे …", 'कान पड़े गुण देय' |
| Postpositions | joined to the word: जीवके, बारेमें, आत्माको, सबसु, टेबलमें; also after an English word in Devanagari (ऑर्डर्‌में) or in Latin letters (JNUके) | `दादाजीके चरणमें` |
| Visible virama | a virama inside a word before a postposition stays visible: type ZERO WIDTH NON-JOINER (U+200C; `&zwnj;` in markdown) after it: लोगन्‌कु, ग्रंथन्‌को, भगवान्‌की, अपन्‌ने, साइंस्‌को, ऑर्डर्‌में | visible in 146 of about 160 such places in the exemplar; the rest (लिफ्ट्कु, हार्ट्में, उपनिषद्को, पांडवन्कु …) are printed as conjuncts, an inconsistency |
| English words in Hindi syntax | in Devanagari, with a final virama when the English word ends in a consonant (ऑर्डर्, इंडक्टिव्, प्रोडक्ट्, कॉन्शियसनेस्, सब्स्टेंस्, फिलॉसॉफिकल्, गॅजेट्, डिज़ाइन्), none on vowel-final words (क्वॉलिटी, फिलॉसफी) or on fully naturalised words (टेबल, चार्ट, मोबाइल, नंबर, सेमिनार, कॉलेज, नेटवर्क); ऑ for the vowel of *organize*, ॅ for that of *gadget*, nukta for z (ऑर्गेनाइज़्, साइज़्) | `मैं ऑडियंस् ऑर्गेनाइज़् नहीं करूँ, टॉपिक् ऑर्गेनाइज़् करूँ.` |
| English sentences and set phrases | in Latin letters, English spelling and punctuation, inside the Hindi paragraph | `So let us hope for good.`; `How your soul can be destined to go to USA?` |
| Sanskrit quotations | bold, in double quotes, followed at once by the source in brackets, regular weight | **"प्रकाशाश्रयवद्वा तेजस्त्वात्॥"** (ब्र.सू.३.२.२८) |
| Sources | one form each (the exemplar varies): ब्र.सू., त.दी.नि. (with kārikā number), भ.गी., बृहद.उप., छान्दो.उप., तैत्ति.उप., श्वेता.उप., मुण्ड.उप., ब्रह्मबिन्दूपनिषद्, अमरकोश (with varga), न्यायदर्शन, श्रीदक्षिणामूर्त्यष्टकम् (शंकराचार्य), श्रीमच्छंकरदिग्विजय, उपक्रमश्लोक-N for the speaker's own verses; numbers with full stops (तैत्ति.उप.३.१) | `(बृहद.उप.३.७.२२)` |
| Digits | Devanagari in verse numbers, sources, the index, the title page and the contents; Arabic for the folio and where the speaker counts or calculates (1, 2, 3 …; 82 फिलॉसफीज़; 12 पूर्वोत्तर भाव) | `॥१२॥`, `(भ.गी.७.४)` |

## Markdown source for the build

```markdown
---
booklet: hindi
title: "प्रतिबिम्बवाद"
subtitle: "( जीवात्माके स्वरूप पर विशेष प्रवचन )"
occasion:
  - "वल्लभ सुखधाम · गोस्वामी श्रीपुरुषोत्तमजी"
  - "प्राकट्योत्सव २०२६"
speaker: "गोस्वामी श्याम मनोहरजी"
---

* * *

* * *

{{अनुक्रमणिका}}

| न वेद्म्यात्मन्यात्मानं परमात्मानं कुतो न्वहम् ॥
| प्रियता मयि चान्येषु विषयश्चाश्रयो बृहत् ॥१॥
:::

::: verse-prose
तथाहि

१. "एकएव हि भूतात्मा …" २. "यथा ह्ययं …"
:::

::: colophon
| इति गोस्वामिश्रीदीक्षितात्मजेन श्याम मनोहरेण
| निर्मिताः
| प्रतिबिम्बादिवादीयाः उपक्रमश्लोकाः
:::

# खण्ड १ — उपक्रम: अवतारवादावली और प्रतिबिम्बवादको परिचय

अपनो ये उत्सव यहाँ कितने सालन्&zwnj;सु चल रह्यो हे, …

::: figure
![](charts/chart_1.png){width=3.4in}
:::

… जैसे एक लाइट. … सारे रूममें फैले हे—**"प्रकाशाश्रयवद्वा तेजस्त्वात्॥"** (ब्र.सू.३.२.२८)की तरह.

* * *

::: pada-heading
(वल्लभकुल बधाई पद)
:::

::: pada
जुग-जुग राज करो श्री गोकुल, जुग-जुग राज करो!

ये सुख भजन प्रताप ताप तेज ते,
:::

* * *

{{उद्धृत वचन}}
```

- The YAML block makes the title page. `* * *` starts a new page; two in a row leave a blank page. `# खण्ड N — …` starts a section on a new page by itself (do not put `* * *` before it).
- `{{अनुक्रमणिका}}` becomes the contents (from the `#` headings; `contents-pages: true` in the YAML adds page numbers). `{{उद्धृत वचन}}` becomes the index of every quotation written `**"…"** (source)`, with the page numbers it has in the built PDF. A bold quotation without a source is not indexed (the exemplar does the same for "श्रुतं हरति पापानि").
- Page markers `<!-- p.N -->` (as in the exemplar copy) are allowed and ignored.
- Build: `python3 tools/build_booklet.py SOURCE.md OUTDIR`. Check first: `python3 tools/lint_booklet.py SOURCE.md`.

## Workflow, from recording to booklet

1. **Transcribe** with the Sarvam pipeline (`vallabh_vani/tools/`: `sarvam_batch.py`, `saaras:v4`, verbatim, diarized, language `hi-IN` or `unknown`, the series' key terms), then `transcript_from_json.py` for the review transcript and the condensed turns. Run the ten-minute idiom test above.
2. **Collect the speaker's materials**: title and subtitle, venue and occasion, his introductory verses, his chart, the pada sung at the end, and the printed texts his quotations come from. Ask the owner for whatever is missing.
3. **Edit** section by section: punctuation, paragraphs (a new paragraph at each turn of the exposition, every 80–150 words), sections at the real joints of the argument (nine for two hours in the exemplar), quotations found, checked against print, set in bold and sourced; English set as above; the idiom kept. Work in chunks and check after each that every `# खण्ड` heading occurs once.
4. **Lint, build, look**: `lint_booklet.py`, then `build_booklet.py`; look at the title page, contents, the verse pages, two section openings, the chart pages, the pada and the index; compare them with the exemplar pages.
5. **Deliver** `<Title>_<year>.docx` and `.pdf` (`-rev-N` when the owner asks for a revision) and a short closing report: pages, sections, quotations indexed and checked, what remains unresolved (page and opening words), what the owner must supply or decide.

## Where the exemplar is inconsistent with itself (for the owner's decision)

- **Index**: the text of item ५ stands on the line below its number, and from item ५ on whole items (source and page included) are bold; items ९ and १० are the same quotation with the same page (७०) although it occurs on pp. 70 and 72; item २ opens its source bracket with a space (`( 'श्रीदक्षिणामूर्त्यष्टकम्' शंकराचार्य)`). A quotation cut to its opening words has only the opening quotation mark, a complete one both: the generated index does the same, lists every occurrence with its own page, and keeps one weight (quotation bold, source and page regular).
- **Contents and headings differ in wording**: contents `प्रतिबिम्बवाद को परिचय` / heading `प्रतिबिम्बवादको परिचय`; `गुण-विस्तार को विचार` / `गुण-विस्तारको विचार`; `विज्ञान को दृष्टिकोण` / `विज्ञानको दृष्टिकोण`; `शाङ्करमतके` / `शाङ्करमत के`; contents `अणु, मध्यम और विभु-विचार`, heading `अणु, मध्यम और विभु` (and `परिमाण—` without a space). The generated contents repeats the headings.
- **Section openings** are placed by hand with empty lines, so the heading stands between 1.2 and 2.8 in from the top and the text begins between 3.2 and 6.0 in (pp. 23, 34 high; p. 50 low); the template places every opening like p. 10.
- **Sources** are abbreviated two ways (बृहद.उ. / बृहद.उप., तैत्ति.उ. / तैत्ति.उप.) and numbered two ways (३/१ beside ३.७.२२).
- The colophon prints दीतितात्मजेन where दीक्षितात्मजेन is meant (p. 9); item ११ of the तथाहि list (Bhagavadgītā 15.7) is printed ममॅवांशो for ममैवांशो (p. 6).
- यहाँ/यहां, वहाँ/वहां, हूँ/हूं both occur; some verse lines end in the typed pair `।।` instead of ॥; straight and curly quotation marks alternate; ऐ is typed as ए + े; one anusvāra is a combining dot (प्रमाणं, p. 9); one double space (p. 64).
- **The PDF's text layer is broken.** *Microsoft Print to PDF* kept a character map that lacks ि, ई and ऐ and drops every conjunct, so प्रतिबिम्बवाद is stored as "त ब बवाद": the PDF cannot be searched, copied from, read aloud, or read by any text tool (Claude included). Export from Word with *File → Save As → PDF* (or build with `build_booklet.py`), and keep the .docx with the PDF.
