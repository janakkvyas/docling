# Kapila Gītā (Gosvāmī Śrī Śyām Manoharjī) — closing reports

Fourteen verbatim reading editions, 2022-01-30 to 2022-03-03, made per `SPEC.md` from the Sarvam transcripts in `sources/` and built with `tools/build_final.py`; 323 pages in all, every edition lint-clean. Each edition covers its session from start to end: section by section, the edited text is within a few per cent of the transcript's length (verse blocks and glosses aside). Verses were restored only against e-texts of the Sanskrit (GRETIL, vedabase); the long Subodhinī and Nibandha passages the speaker reads aloud have no e-text to check against and stay ‹श्रवणाधारित› throughout.

Across the series the recogniser has three recurring faults, all flagged where they occur: it sometimes printed an English rendering where the speaker was reading Sanskrit, Braj or Hindi (kept as transcribed with ‹?› or replaced by ‹…›, with a note); it inserted keyterm names, chiefly "Devahūti" and "Kardama", out of context (dropped or restored, e.g. ‹krīḍā›, ‹daivāt›); and it heard the students' address "JJ" as "DJ", "Jiji" or "Devahūti" (restored to "JJ"). These passages should be checked against the audio.
