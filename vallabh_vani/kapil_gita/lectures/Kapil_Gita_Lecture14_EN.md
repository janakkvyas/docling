# 2. Manas is not "mind": the two verses on manas and Mahāprabhuji's division of their pādas

*[00:00:54]*

**Speaker:** Jai Śrī Kṛṣṇa. Up till now we have understood the *ahaṅkāra*; now we are coming to *manas*. *Manas* is not mind, because the mind is total *manas*, *buddhi*, *citta*, *ahaṅkāra*; that's why *manas* cannot be translated into mind. It is a different entity. Mind is total of *manas*, *buddhi*, *citta* and *ahaṅkāra*. So:

>

>
> [That mind is known as Aniruddha, one who cannot be restrained by any other factors, the Governor of all the *indriya*s; it is *śāradendīvaraśyāma*, just like blue lotus which blooms at night and blue in colour; and that the mind one has to slowly, by controlling the other factors.]

*Vaikārikāt* means: from *sāttvika ahaṅkāra* gets transformed into *manas-tattva*, and it acts in form of *saṅkalpa*. Exact translation is not there, but we can call it a determining power. *Yad vidur aniruddhākhyaṁ hṛṣīkāṇām adhīśvaram*: and that mind is known as Aniruddha, one who cannot be restrained by any other factors; that is known as one, and it is the governing, Governor of all the *indriya*s — sense organs, sense, motor organs, all the *indriya*s, whatever organs are there; the controlling power rest in hand of mind. *Manasa utpattim āha — vaikārikād iti. Ardhena utpattiḥ* ‹श्रवणाधारित›. *Utpatti* means from first line; *ardha-śloka* means one line. Four, because when we, in *anuṣṭup*, *vaikārikād vikurvāṇāt* — we take the pause over here, so that makes one *caraṇa*. *Vaikārikād vikurvāṇān manas-tattvam ajāyata, yat-saṅkalpa-vikalpābhyāṁ vartate kāma-sambhavaḥ.* So the — Mahāprabhuji describes that from one line, *vaikārikād vikurvāṇān manas-tattvam ajāyata*, from here the production is described. *Ardhena ādhyātmikaṁ lakṣaṇaṁ ca* ‹श्रवणाधारित› — *ardhena*: *yat-saṅkalpa-vikalpābhyāṁ vartate kāma-sambhavaḥ*; from this line *ādhyātmika* definition is given. *Caraṇa-trayeṇa ādhidaivikaṁ lakṣaṇam* ‹श्रवणाधारित› means *yad vidur hy aniruddhākhyaṁ hṛṣīkāṇām adhīśvaram*, this two, and third, *śāradendīvaraśyāmam*; from this three *caraṇa* or *pāda* *ādhidaivika svarūpa* is described. # Brief Summary

**Content.** The session passes from *ahaṅkāra* to *manas* and *buddhi* on the text of Śrīmad Bhāgavata 3.26.27–30 read with Mahāprabhuji's Subodhinī. *Manas* is not "mind": the mind is the total of *manas*, *buddhi*, *citta* and *ahaṅkāra*. The Subodhinī divides the two verses on *manas* into *utpatti* (half a verse), *ādhyātmika lakṣaṇa* (half a verse), *ādhidaivika lakṣaṇa* (three pādas) and *ādhibhautika lakṣaṇa* (the remaining pāda), and the speaker advises the students to tabulate these four heads for *citta*, *ahaṅkāra*, *manas* and *buddhi* so as to understand and remember by contrast. *Manas* is produced from the *sāttvika ahaṅkāra* when time sets the guṇas vibrating, *bhagavad-vīrya* is to be understood behind every step of creation, and the word *tattva* excludes the Upaniṣadic *annamaya* view and any imputed defect. Its *svarūpa-lakṣaṇa* is *saṅkalpa* (determination, assisting the motor organs) and *vikalpa* (alternatives, assisting the sense organs and the *buddhi*); its *kārya-lakṣaṇa* is *kāma-janakatva*. Its *adhideva* is Aniruddha, the *pālaka* and *īśvara* of the *indriya*s, unrestrainable and powerful; the purpose of the *ādhidaivika* definition is *pratīkāra*, which in *bhakti-mārga* is *bhagavad-upāsanā*, never fighting with one's own mind. *Śāradendīvaraśyāma* names a flawless and lovable form which is to be served, and the *kartavyopadeśa* is that by continuous, affectionate *bhagavat-sevā* the defects of the mind recede by themselves. The *ādhibhautika* definition, *saṁrādhyaṁ yogibhiḥ śanaiḥ*, leads to Mahāprabhuji's *devatā tu na śanakair ārādhyā* and the three causes of *bhajana*: *dīrghakāla*, *ādara* and *nairantarya*.
