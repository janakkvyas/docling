# 2. Bhakti Like a Kalpavṛkṣa: the Four Kinds of Bhakta, the Jñānī Bhakta and the Jijñāsu

*[00:04:19]*

**Speaker:** Yes, definitely, definitely.

**Listener 2:** *Sir, Jeje kā ek sentence hai book meṁ, ki bhakti kalpavṛkṣa kī tarah hai aur vo icchit phal detā hai. **Speaker:** Means — the main point is this, in devotion, that God has also said; Bhāgavata is saying:

> किमलभ्यं भगवति प्रसन्ने श्रीनिकेतने ।  
> तथापि तत्परा राजन्न हि वाञ्छन्ति किञ्चन ॥  
>
> *kim alabhyaṁ bhagavati prasanne śrī-niketane |*  
> *tathāpi tat-parā rājan na hi vāñchanti kiñcana ||* (Śrīmad Bhāgavata 10.39.2)
>
> [What is unattainable when Bhagavān, the abode of Śrī, is pleased? Even so, O king, those devoted to him do not want anything.]

*Kalpavṛkṣa ke nīce — bhakti just like kalpavṛkṣa hai; isliye Bhagavān Gītā meṁ bhī kahte haiṁ* [under the *kalpavṛkṣa* — *bhakti* is just like a *kalpavṛkṣa*; that is why Bhagavān says in the Gītā also]:

> चतुर्विधा भजन्ते मां जनाः सुकृतिनोऽर्जुन ।  
> आर्तो जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी ज्ञानी च भरतर्षभ ॥  
>
> *catur-vidhā bhajante māṁ janāḥ sukṛtino 'rjuna |*  
> *ārto jijñāsur arthārthī jñānī ca bharatarṣabha ||* (Bhagavad Gītā 7.16)
>
> [Four kinds of people of good deeds worship me, Arjuna: the *ārta*, the *jijñāsu*, the *arthārthī* and the *jñānī*, O best of the Bharatas.]

That four types of — there are four types of *bhakta*: one who are *ārta*, means disturbed — again, totally disturbed; *arthārthī*, those who want some *sampatti*; some are *jijñāsu* — they want to have, they are inquisitive, they want to have a true knowledge, *jijñāsu*; and *jñānī* also. But stand of Mahāprabhuji is that — when you — Bhagavān also says, just like *bhakti* — Bhagavān also says:

> ये यथा मां प्रपद्यन्ते तांस्तथैव भजाम्यहम् ।  
>
> *ye yathā māṁ prapadyante tāṁs tathaiva bhajāmy aham |* (Bhagavad Gītā 4.11)
>
> [How they become *prapanna* before me, how they interact with me, how they look at me — same way, in response, I also behave in the same manner.]

How they become *prapanna* before me, how they interact with me, how they look at me and how they interact with me — same way, in response, I also behave in the same manner. So here *bhakti* also: if you are *sakāma*, will provide — may provide, may not provide — your *kāmanā*, *sampatti* also; or your *jijñāsā* — if you want to have knowledge, want to have knowledge from Bhagavān — so according to that. If you see Jeje's lecture, he has said, according to *stuti* also you will find: those who are *sakāma*, *arthārthī*, will always praise Bhagavān — *he Lakṣmī-dātā, he dhana-dātā*; those who are *jijñāsu* will say *he jñāna-dātā*. That — that Bhagavān also gives in response to this, their feeling; whatever *bhāvanā* is there, Bhagavān also give response to them. So *bhakti* is just like *kalpavṛkṣa*. But Mahāprabhuji says: this *bhakti*, in a true sense, is *māhātmya-jñāna-pūrvas tu sudṛḍhaḥ sarvato 'dhikaḥ snehaḥ* ‹श्रवणाधारित›. This is *sudṛḍha* — because in other, other way, what happens: if you get anything — if you want anything from Bhagavān or *bhakti*, you will get it; so then *bhakti* will increase; if you don't get, that *bhakti* will decrease. So that is *sopādhikatā*. And *su-*, *sudṛḍha* and *sarvato 'dhika* — so it won't be that *dṛḍha*. *Sudṛḍha* and *sarvato 'dhika bhakti*, which you want — doesn't want anything: you don't want anything from him; one does not want anything from Bhagavān — that *bhakti* is the true *bhakti*.

# 4. Naikātmatām: Devī Jīvas Who Refuse Oneness — the Prime Minister and the Mumbai Complaints
