# Kapila Gītā, Lectures 1–15 — Closing Reports

Series: fifteen sessions in English by Gosvāmī Śrī Śyām Manoharjī on the Kapila Gītā (Śrīmad Bhāgavata 3.25–33), 2022-10-30 to 2023-03-05 (Lecture 15 is the revision session). Each edition keeps the speaker's own words and vocabulary; editing is limited to punctuation, paragraphing, section headings with start timestamps, speaker labels, and the restoration of quoted verses to their received text where an e-text allowed verification (Bhāgavata and Gītā against vedabase/GRETIL; Upaniṣads and the ṣoḍaśa-granthas against sanskritdocuments.org and others). Mahāprabhuji's Subodhinī, which the speaker reads aloud at length, has no reliable e-text, so its sentences are given as heard in IAST and flagged ‹श्रवणाधारित› for checking against print. Series-wide: the recogniser's token "JJ" (the teacher the speaker cites for pravacanas and a ghazal) is rendered throughout as the honorific *Jeje*, a reading the speaker should confirm; names and terms are spelled uniformly (Mahāprabhuji, Gosāīṁjī, Ṭhākurjī, Sāṅkhya, Jai Śrī Kṛṣṇa). Where the recogniser emitted its own English paraphrase in place of a Sanskrit sentence the speaker read, or a burst of its key-term list, the editions say so and mark the gap.
