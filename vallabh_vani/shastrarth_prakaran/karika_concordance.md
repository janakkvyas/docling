Harishankar Shastri, published by J. G. Shah for the Narayandas Jethanand Trust, Bombay 1943. archive.org: `jhzf-tattvartha-deepa-nibandha-shastrartha-prakarana-pr` (<https://archive.org/details/jhzf-tattvartha-deepa-nibandha-shastrartha-prakarana-pr>). "Leaf *n*" below is the scan image `https://archive.org/download/jhzf-tattvartha-deepa-nibandha-shastrartha-prakarana-pr/page/n<n>.jpg`; "p." is the page number printed on that image. The red mūla pages are leaves 103–109 (pp. २–८); mūla p. १ (kārikās 1–10) is missing from the scan (leaf 101 is a blank verso, leaf 102 a photograph), so kārikās 1–10 were read in the commentary (printed page = leaf − 109) and in the Gujarati-translation section (leaves 352–356 = pp. २७–३१). The edition has 104 kārikās (55 and 56 share one number, printed ॥ ५५ ॥ ५६ ॥). archive.org's `page_numbers.json` for this item is wrong; every page cited here was read off the image.
2. **1904 Mumbai (Gyan Sagar Press).** *Saprakāśa Tattvadīpanibandha* with Puruṣottama's Āvaraṇabhaṅga and a Braj-bhāṣā ṭīkā, ed. Gokuladāsa, Mumbai 1904. archive.org: `gjru_saprakasha-tattva-dipa-nibandha-avarana-bhanga-purushottama-by-vallabhachar` (<https://archive.org/details/gjru_saprakasha-tattva-dipa-nibandha-avarana-bhanga-purushottama-by-vallabhachar>); printed page [*x*] = leaf − 17. It numbers 108 units. 3. Also seen: Shyam Manohar Goswami, *Vallabhavedāntanibandhasaṅgraha* (archive.org `vallabhvedantanibandhasangrahshyammanohargoswami_202003_937_W`), which cites kārikā 104 as त.दी.नि.प्र. १।१०४ and quotes kārikās 7–9 under the 1943 numbers.

**Confidence.** **VERIFIED**: read legibly in the scan image, not just in archive.org's OCR. **PROBABLE**: the scan or OCR is noisy but metre and context leave the reading clear. **UNVERIFIED**: not found in either edition, or not checked.

**Conventions.** Kārikā numbers are the 1943 numbers. Half-lines are ab / cd / ef, as the series writes "BhP 1.2.11cd". The Devanagari and IAST under each kārikā are given as they should stand in a series verse block (SPEC: । after every line but the last, ॥ after the last; a three-line verse keeps । until its last line, as the 1943 commentary prints it). In the locus tables, "Citation now" is the parenthesis the lecture gives at that spot (— = none); "Flag" = the locus carries ‹श्रवणाधारित›; "Against 1943" lists the words that differ, ignoring hyphens, spacing, daṇḍa style, avagraha, anusvāra versus class nasal, and pausal -ḥ versus sandhi -s; "confirmed" means a ‹ › conjecture matches the print. Line ranges such as `sp01:46–52` are whole verse blocks (verse lines and gloss).

- **Prakāśa sentences quoted:** 93 sentences in 16 passages (on kārikās 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 23, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 54). All **VERIFIED** in the 1943 scans (PROBABLE 0, UNVERIFIED 0).
- **Numbers.** No lecture cites a number that contradicts the 1943 edition. The numbers given in L01 (1), L04 (7), L05p2 (8, 9, 10, 104; Prakāśa on 54), L07 (12, 23) and L10 (1, for *rūpa-nāma-vibhedena*) are right, as are the prose references "sixth śloka" (L01), "twelfth śloka" (L07) and "twenty-sixth śloka" (L12). The L11/L12 labels "26" and "27" sit on two-line blocks that straddle verses (26cd + 27ab, 27cd + 28ab). They match the units of the 1943 commentary (and the 1904 numbering, #30/#31) but not the verse division, so cite them as 26cd–27ab and 27cd–28ab (Part C). Most other verse blocks carry no number yet; C.2 gives the number for each. The 1904 numbers run ahead of 1943 (15 = #16, 23 = #26, 42 = #46, 44 = #48, 104 = #108) and split several verses, so a number taken from the 1904 print would be wrong for the series.
- **Kārikā wording that differs from the print:** L02 has the third line of 6 as *tam enaiva mayā tarhi* (print *krameṇaiva mayātra hi*; L01 has it right and can drop its flag). L09 has 12ab as *sūryādi-rūpaṁ* (print *sūryādirūpadhṛg*, which also fixes the metre). L11 and L12 drop *hy* in *hy avibhaktaṁ* (26cd) and *hy abhūt* (27ab); the Prakāśa comments on that very *hi*. L12 writes *vispuliṅgā* for *visphuliṅgā* (28cd) and *vyavahāras tāmasaḥ*‹?› for *vyavahāras tridhā mataḥ* (30cd). L06 has *pūrva-saṁśaya-vārakam* (8ab, print *sandeha*) and *dvayaṁ pramāṇam … kathaiva* (10, print *pramā … tathaiva*); L07 has *dvipa‹ttva›* (10ab, print *dviparatvād*); L02 has *pūrva-saṁśaya-vārakaṁ parihṛtam* (8ab) and the dropped-kārikā fragments *devā* (2cd, print *daivāt*, 1904 *daivāḥ*) and *bhagavat-tātparya ājñā vicārita* (3ab, print *bhagavacchāstram ājñāya vicārya ca*). The three-line verses 6 and 8 should keep । until the last line (L01, L03p2).
- **Prakāśa wording that differs from the print:** the Prakāśa on 7 has no *sat* (L02, L03p2 and L05p1 read *ekavākyatāpannaṁ sat pramājanakam*); L01's Prakāśa on 1 has *jīvair*, not ‹jīvena›, and no *kartuṁ*; L01's ‹*svamate*› before *paribhāṣām āha* is not in the print; L12 *brahma bhavitum arhati*‹?› is *brahma-bhūtāḥ*, and *etasmin* is *ekasmin*; L09 ‹*muhyanti*› is *mugdhā bhavanti*; L10 ‹*nivartate*›‹?› is *kiṁ bhavet*, ‹*aṅgīkāryam*› is *urarīkāryam*, and *ajñānaṁ, bhramaḥ ityādi-śabdāḥ* lacks *asad*; L08 ‹*sa*›*pravṛttim* is *svapravṛttim* and *cādvaita-vaināśika-nirākaraṇa-prakaraṇe* is *cāgre vaināśika-prakriyā-nirākaraṇe*; L06 and L07 have smaller slips listed in Part B. Many ‹ › conjectures are confirmed by the print, and their flags can go (marked "confirmed").

- *anantamūrti tad brahma* also opens kārikā 71 (अनन्तमूर्ति तद्ब्रह्म कूटस्थं चलमेव च ।); every lecture occurrence is kārikā 26cd.
- *śuddhaṁ bhajanaṁ vārayanti* (L08) echoes kārikā 15ef (*yathākathañcit kṛṣṇasya bhajanaṁ vārayanti hi*) but is the Prakāśa on 23 (P10.2).
- *akhaṇḍaṁ kṛṣṇavat sarvaṁ yathā tattva-nirūpitam* (L06) is not in the Śāstrārtha Prakaraṇa; the Prakāśa on 13–14 quotes its first words as said later in the Nibandha (वक्ष्यति च "अखण्डं कृष्णवत्सर्वम्").
- *śāstre skandhe prakaraṇe 'dhyāye vākye pade 'kṣare* (L04) is the Bhāgavatārtha Prakaraṇa verse; the 1943 Āvaraṇabhaṅga (p. १५, leaf 124, OCR) quotes it ending एकार्थं सप्तधा जानन्नविरोधेन मुच्यते.
- *vidhinā tv ekavākyatvāt stutyarthena vidhīnāṁ syuḥ* (L04, L05p1) is Jaimini's sūtra, quoted in the Āvaraṇabhaṅga on 7, not in the Prakāśa.
