## Vallabh Vani

This fork also holds the Vallabh Vani work (transcription, translation and publication of Gosvāmī Śrī Śyām Manoharjī's discourses), independent of the Docling package. Before any task on it, read `vallabh_vani/README.md` and `vallabh_vani/PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

Standing rules from the owner for that work (restated 2026-10-24):

- **Translations are verbatim**, in the format of the earlier deliveries: the series briefs (`vallabh_vani/shastrarth_prakaran/SPEC.md` for Hindi lectures translated into English, `vallabh_vani/kapil_gita/SPEC.md` for lectures given in English) and `vallabh_vani/tools/build_final.py`. Take nothing from published works: no wording, renderings, conventions or layout from published translations or from the booklets in `vallabh_vani/booklets/`, which are reference only for making a booklet when the owner asks for one.
- **Deliver Word files only** (`.docx`): never send the owner a PDF or a markdown file. Markdown is the working source kept in this repository; PDFs are built only to check the layout.
- **Check whose lecture it is.** The project is Gosvāmī Śrī Śyām Manoharjī's discourses. The Kapila Gītā sessions in `vallabh_vani/kapil_gita/` are by Dr. Dharmendra Jhala and were transcribed by mistake; confirm the speaker of any new recording with the owner before working on it.
