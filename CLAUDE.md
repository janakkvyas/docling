## Vallabh Vani

This fork also holds the Vallabh Vani work (transcription, translation and publication of Gosvāmī Śrī Śyām Manoharjī's discourses), independent of the Docling package. Before any task on it, read `vallabh_vani/README.md` and `vallabh_vani/PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

Standing rules from the owner for that work:

- **Deliver Word files only** (`.docx`): never send the owner a PDF or a markdown file. Markdown is the working source kept in this repository; PDFs are built only to check the layout.
- **Every project follows the owner's sample publications**: the form, typography and conventions of the product it belongs to, as the briefs in `vallabh_vani/booklets/` describe them (Hindi pravachan booklet, English stuti book, English discourse booklet).
